My Baby Won't Sleep Without White Noise. I Tested 4 Machines — The $22 One Won.

Two weeks, four sound machines, one very opinionated 4-month-old.

OK so here's the thing nobody told me before I had a baby: newborns are LOUD sleepers. They grunt, they squeak, they make noises that sound like a tiny velociraptor. And if you're a first-time mom who bolts upright at every sound like I was, you need white noise. Not for the baby — for YOU.

I bought four of the most popular sound machines on Amazon and ran them through a completely unscientific but very real two-week test. Here's what my baby (and my anxiety) decided.

The Lineup

  • Yogasleep Hushh — $22, portable, 3 sounds, baby-specific — 4.6★ (15,000+ reviews)
  • Hatch Rest 2nd Gen — $69, WiFi + app controlled, 10 sounds, night light, toddler-ready — 4.7★ (8,000+ reviews)
  • Momcozy Portable — $29, 12 sounds, 15-hour battery, USB-C — 4.7★ (3,500+ reviews)
  • LectroFan Classic — $35, 20 sounds, no lights, basic as it gets — 4.6★ (45,000+ reviews)

The $22 Underdog: Yogasleep Hushh

This little guy is about the size of a softball and has exactly three buttons. I clipped it to the car seat handle and it stayed put. The "shush" sound is everything — it mimics the sound babies hear in the womb, and my daughter knocked out in under 5 minutes when I used it during a fussy evening. Battery lasts about 8 hours on a charge, which got me through two nights of hotel sleep during a weekend trip. For $22, I bought a second one to keep in the diaper bag permanently.

Downside: only three sounds (shush, ocean, rain). If your baby needs variety, this isn't it. But honestly? We only ever used shush.

The Status Symbol: Hatch Rest 2

The Hatch is gorgeous. It looks like a minimalist sculpture. The app lets you program routines — red light at bedtime, green light when it's OK to wake up, different sounds at different times. I felt like a tech genius setting it up.

Here's what actually happened: my baby doesn't care about the app. The red light confused her. The "time to rise" green light feature is for toddlers, not infants. And the app disconnected from WiFi twice in one week, which meant the sound just... stopped. At 2 AM. Not great.

I think this will be amazing when my daughter is 2 or 3 and understands the color cues. For a newborn? A very expensive white noise machine that occasionally decides to update its firmware instead of playing ocean sounds.

The Surprise Favorite: Momcozy Portable

I bought this on a whim because it was on lightning deal for $22 and I have a problem. It ended up being the one we use every single night. Twelve sounds (more than we need), a warm night light that doesn't confuse anyone, and USB-C charging — meaning I don't need a special cable just for the sound machine. The battery genuinely lasts 15 hours. I forgot to charge it for three days and it was still going.

The shush sound on the Momcozy isn't quite as good as the Yogasleep — it's slightly more mechanical-sounding. But the overall package is better. At $29 full price (or frequently on sale), this is the one I recommend to every new mom who asks.

The No-Nonsense Workhorse: LectroFan Classic

Twenty sound options. Zero lights. No app. Plug it in, press a button, it runs until you turn it off. This is the sound machine for parents who want exactly one thing: noise that blocks out the garbage truck at 6 AM. The fan sounds are genuinely convincing — my husband thought I'd left the bathroom fan on for three days.

It's not portable (no battery). It's not cute. But it's built like a tank, and with 45,000 reviews averaging 4.6 stars, it's clearly doing something right. This lives in our nursery permanently. The Momcozy travels with us.

What I Actually Recommend

If you have $25: get the Yogasleep Hushh. Clip it everywhere, travel with it, buy a second one.

If you have $30: get the Momcozy. Best all-rounder with a battery that won't quit.

If you want the toddler-to-big-kid upgrade path: the Hatch Rest 2 makes sense long-term. Just know the first year is mostly just an expensive white noise machine.

If you want set-it-and-forget-it: the LectroFan will outlive your child's noise machine needs and probably your next home renovation.

🛒 Where to buy: All four are available on Amazon. The Yogasleep Hushh and Momcozy frequently go on Lightning Deals — put them in your cart and wait for the price drop notification.
