The Self-Care Products That Kept Me Sane During Maternity Leave (On 4 Hours Of Sleep)

Week 6 hit me like a truck. I'd been running on adrenaline and now I was running on empty. These small things made a real difference.

The first five weeks with my daughter, I was running on pure adrenaline. Then week 6 hit. My husband went back to work. The meal train stopped. The texts checking in slowed to a trickle. And I was alone with a baby who screamed from 5 PM to 9 PM every single night.

I hit a wall so hard I called my mom sobbing at 10 AM on a Tuesday and couldn't form actual words. She showed up an hour later with a bag of groceries, a dry shampoo, and a pair of noise-canceling earbuds. "You need to take care of you too," she said. "The baby needs a mom who isn't completely depleted."

She was right. Here are the small luxuries that actually helped me feel human again.

Loop Earplugs: The Colic Survival Tool

During the witching hours, when my daughter was purple with rage and absolutely nothing would soothe her, I'd pop in my Loop Engage earplugs ($35). They don't block sound completely — they just take the edge off. I could still hear her, still respond to her, but the screaming no longer felt like it was physically drilling into my skull. The difference was immediate: I was calmer, which meant she calmed down faster too. Every new mom should have a pair in her hospital bag.

Small Luxuries That Added Up

A Stanley 40oz tumbler ($45) sounds ridiculous — it's a cup. But when you're trapped under a contact-napping baby for 3 hours, having ice-cold water within arm's reach without getting up is genuinely life-improving. I drank twice as much water with this thing. The Barefoot Dreams robe ($98) was my uniform for the first 3 months — soft enough that it didn't irritate my C-section incision, presentable enough for unexpected visitors. My wireless earbuds ($129) kept me company during those endless middle-of-the-night feeds — podcasts, audiobooks, anything to keep me awake and feel like I was still connected to the adult world. And a tube of caffeine eye cream ($16) made me look like I'd slept at least 2 of the 4 hours I actually got.

The $18 magnesium supplement was recommended by my midwife for postpartum anxiety. I noticed the difference within a week — the 3 AM doom-scrolling stopped. Your results may vary, but it's worth asking your provider about.

🛒 Where to buy: Loop earplugs, Stanley tumbler, Barefoot Dreams, wireless earbuds, magnesium — all on Amazon.
