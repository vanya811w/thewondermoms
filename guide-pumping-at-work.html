Pumping At Work: How I Made It 14 Months Without Losing My Mind (Or My Supply)

I pumped in a converted supply closet for 14 months. Here's the gear that actually got me through it.

When I returned to work at 12 weeks postpartum, my office's "lactation room" was a storage closet with a chair, a mini fridge from 2003, and a sign that said "Please Knock." A colleague walked in on me on day 2. I considered quitting — both my job and breastfeeding. Instead, I bought the right gear and built a system that worked for 14 months.

The Pump: Spectra S1 Plus

The Spectra S1 Plus ($216) was covered by my insurance and I'd choose it again even if it wasn't. It's hospital-grade but portable — the rechargeable battery lasts for about 4 pumping sessions, which meant I could pump in my car during field visits. It's a closed system, so I sold it for $80 when I was done. The Spectra is quieter than the Medela and gentler than the Baby Buddha. If your insurance offers multiple pump options, pick the Spectra.

Hands-Free Setup

The Kindred Bravely pumping bra ($42) is the one that actually held the flanges in place. I tried 3 cheaper versions from Amazon and they all sagged after 2 washes. This one holds its shape and doubles as a nursing bra. Combined with the Legendairy Milk collection cups ($59) that fit inside a regular bra instead of dangling bottles, I could actually answer emails while pumping instead of staring at the wall.

Transport and Cleaning

The Ceres Chill milk chiller ($69) replaced my bulky cooler bag and ice packs. It's a double-walled steel container that keeps milk cold for 20+ hours. I'd pump directly into collection bottles, pour into the Ceres Chill, and everything stayed at safe temperature until I got home. The Medela sanitizing bags ($6 for 5) let me steam-clean pump parts in the office microwave in 90 seconds instead of washing in the break room sink while making awkward eye contact with my boss. And if I'd been traveling for work, I would have used Milk Stork ($59 shipping cooler) to overnight milk home — I used it once for a 3-day conference and it was worth every penny.

🛒 Where to buy: Spectra, Kindred Bravely, Ceres Chill, Medela bags — all on Amazon. Check if your insurance covers the Spectra.
