Sleep Training At 5 Months: The Products That Made It Possible

I swore I'd never sleep train. Then I hadn't slept more than 2 consecutive hours in 5 months. Here's what changed everything.

The turning point was when I found myself googling "can sleep deprivation kill you" at 3 AM. My 5-month-old was waking every 45-90 minutes. I was hallucinating shadows. My husband and I were fighting constantly. Something had to change.

We used the Taking Cara Babies method (modified Ferber) and by night 4, our daughter slept 7 PM to 7 AM. She's been doing it ever since — she's now 14 months old. Here are the products that actually made a difference.

Hatch Rest+: The MVP of Sleep Training

The Hatch Rest+ ($89) is a sound machine, night light, and "okay to wake" clock all in one. During sleep training, the red light signaled "it's still sleep time" and the white noise blocked out the sound of us walking past her door. Now that she's older, the green light tells her when she can get up. The programmable routines in the app mean we never have to manually adjust it. The backup battery even worked during a power outage last month.

SlumberPod: The Privacy Tent

We were sharing a hotel room at my sister-in-law's wedding when we realized our daughter could see us from her pack and play. She was NOT happy about it. A friend recommended the SlumberPod ($179) — it's a blackout tent that goes over a pack and play, creating a private sleep space. It blocks 98% of light and gives you a place to change the baby. We now use it whenever we travel, at grandma's house, and honestly sometimes in our own room when we need to grab something without waking her. It's pricey for what it is, but if you travel more than twice a year it pays for itself in saved sleep.

Other Sleep Essentials

The Nested Bean weighted sleep sack ($35) has a lightly weighted pad on the chest that mimics your hand — it helped our daughter feel secure during the transition from swaddle to sleep sack. The Nanit Pro monitor ($299) let us watch her breathing from our phone without going in the room and accidentally waking her. And the $25 portable blackout blinds with suction cups turned our bright nursery into a cave in 2 minutes flat — essential for daytime naps and summer evenings.
