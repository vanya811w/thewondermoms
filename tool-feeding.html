Feeding Guide by Age

  • Newborn (0-1m): 8-12 feeds/day, every 2-3 hours
  • 1-3 months: 7-9 feeds/day, every 2.5-3.5 hours
  • 3-6 months: 5-7 feeds/day, every 3-4 hours
  • 6-9 months: 4-6 feeds/day + 1-2 solid meals
  • 9-12 months: 3-5 feeds/day + 2-3 solid meals
