I Used 3 Nursing Pillows And Learned The Hard Way That Price Doesn't Predict Comfort

I tandem-fed twins for 14 months. My back has opinions now. Here is what I learned about nursing pillows the hard way so you do not have to.

When I found out I was having twins, I did what any reasonable person would do: I panic-bought everything on every registry list I could find. By the time my due date rolled around, I owned three nursing pillows, two different breast pumps, and approximately 47 swaddles. I figured more stuff equaled more control over a situation that was clearly about to be completely out of control.

Spoiler alert: it did not. But the nursing pillow situation? That I actually figured out.

Here is the thing nobody tells you about nursing pillows. There are approximately 4,000 options on Amazon, and every single one claims to be "ergonomic" and "pediatrician-approved." I bought three of the most popular ones so you do not have to gamble your sore postpartum body on marketing copy. Let us break them down.

The Contenders At A Glance

  • My Brest Friend Deluxe — $49 — 4.8 stars — 18,000+ reviews on Amazon
  • Boppy Original — $39 — 4.7 stars — 25,000+ reviews on Amazon
  • Luna Lullaby Travel Nursing Pillow — $29 — 4.5 stars — 5,000+ reviews on Amazon

My Brest Friend Deluxe — The Surprise Champion ($49)

Okay, I need to be honest with you. When I first pulled this thing out of the box, I laughed. It looks ridiculous. It is basically a foam board strapped around your waist with a buckle. There is zero "nursing pillow aesthetic" here. This is not the cute patterned pillow you see in influencer photos. This is a piece of equipment.

And that is exactly why it works.

The wrap-around design means the pillow stays PUT. You buckle it around your waist, and it does not shift when your baby moves. It does not slide when you adjust positions. It just stays there, doing its job, no drama. With twins, I needed to nurse both babies at the same time or I would have literally never slept, and the Boppy just could not handle the twin gymnastics. My Brest Friend gave me a flat, firm surface at the perfect height that I could position both babies on without one rolling into the other's face.

But the real hero feature? The back support. When you are nursing for 45-minute stretches six to eight times a day, your lower back starts staging a protest. The My Brest Friend has this built-in lumbar support that angles you forward just slightly so you are not hunched over like a gargoyle. I did not realize how much it helped until I switched pillows one day and spent the evening crying on a heating pad.

It also has a little pocket for your phone and a burp cloth. Which sounds gimmicky until you are trapped under a sleeping baby and your water bottle is three feet away.

The downsides: it is bulky. You are not traveling with this thing. It lives in your nursing chair and it stays there. Also, the fabric cover is not the softest thing in the world. I bought a second cover from Amazon for $12 and that solved the problem.

Boppy Original — The Overachiever That Is Actually Just Fine ($39)

The Boppy is the nursing pillow. You know it. Your mom knows it. There are 25,000 Amazon reviews saying it is amazing, and I am here to tell you: it is fine. It is a perfectly fine pillow.

It is basically a C-shaped piece of polyfill wrapped in a cute cover. That is it. And honestly? It works for a lot of people. If you are nursing one baby, have good posture, and do not mind adjusting the pillow every few minutes, you will probably love it. My sister used her Boppy with all three of her kids and swears by it.

But for me, nursing twins, the Boppy was a letdown. The pillow shifts constantly because there is no strap. It compresses down over time so after three months mine was half its original thickness. And because it is so soft, my babies kind of sank into it, which meant I was constantly adjusting their position so they could actually breathe while eating.

Here is the hot take though: the Boppy is actually better as a baby-propper-upper than as a nursing pillow. Once my girls hit about four months and started working on sitting, the Boppy became our go-to for supported sitting practice. I would plop them in it on the play mat and they would practice balancing and reaching for toys. For that purpose? It is genuinely great.

As a nursing pillow though? It is a $39 C-shape with an excellent marketing team. You can do better.

Luna Lullaby Travel Nursing Pillow — The Budget Surprise ($29)

I bought this one on a whim because it was cheap and I needed a second pillow for the living room. I did not expect much. Joke is on me because this $29 pillow outperformed the Boppy in several ways.

The Luna is firmer than the Boppy, which I actually preferred. It has this velvety soft cover that my babies seemed to like more than the Boppy's cotton. And it comes with a little extender strap so you can sort of buckle it around yourself. It is not as secure as the My Brest Friend system, but it is a heck of a lot more stable than the unbuckled Boppy.

At 5,000 reviews on Amazon with a 4.5-star rating, it is not as popular as the big names, but the people who bought it seem to genuinely love it. It is also machine washable, which is basically a requirement for any baby product.

Where it falls short: it is smaller overall, so by the time my girls hit six months, they were kind of hanging off the edges. And because it is firmer, it is not as comfortable for lounging or using as a regular pillow between feedings. It is a tool, not a cloud.

For the price though? This is an incredible value. If you are on a tight budget or need a backup pillow for a second floor of the house, grab this one without hesitation.

The Verdict

If I had to pick one nursing pillow to recommend to a friend (and I have, multiple times now), it is the My Brest Friend without question. Yes, it costs more. Yes, it looks dorky. Yes, your husband will make fun of it. But your back will thank you, your baby will stay latched, and you will not spend every feeding fighting with a pillow that keeps sliding sideways.

The Boppy is fine if someone gifts it to you. Register for it if you want, but know that you will probably end up buying something else by month two. And the Luna Lullaby is a fantastic budget option that punches way above its weight class.

At the end of the day, a nursing pillow is one of those things you do not realize you desperately need until you try to nurse without one. Your arms get tired, your back aches, and your baby gets frustrated. Spend the $49 on the My Brest Friend. Your postpartum body has been through enough.

Where To Buy: My Brest Friend Deluxe ($49) and Boppy Original ($39) are available on Amazon with Prime shipping. The Luna Lullaby Travel Pillow ($29) is also on Amazon and frequently has a 5% coupon available.
