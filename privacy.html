1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

We collect information that you voluntarily provide when you interact with our Services, including:

  • Contact Information: When you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address. When you use our contact form, we collect your name, email address, and any message content you choose to provide.
  • Tool Inputs: When you use our interactive tools (such as the Due Date Calculator, Ovulation Calculator, Baby Feeding Tracker, Pregnancy Weight Tracker, Chinese Gender Predictor, and Baby Name Finder), you may input information such as dates, weights, heights, and preferences. All tool calculations are performed locally in your browser. We do not transmit, store, or have access to the data you enter into these tools.
  • Communications: If you contact us directly via email or our contact form, we may retain records of that correspondence, including your contact details and the content of our communication.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our Site, certain information is collected automatically through standard web technologies:

  • Log Data: Our web server automatically records information that your browser sends whenever you visit a website. This may include your Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser type and version, browser language, the pages of our Site that you visit, the time and date of your visit, the time spent on those pages, referring and exit URLs, and other diagnostic data.
  • Device Information: We may collect information about the device you use to access our Site, including device type, operating system and version, screen resolution, and mobile network information.
  • Usage Data: We may collect aggregated, anonymized information about how you interact with our Site, such as which pages are most frequently visited, how users navigate through the Site, and which features are used most often. This data does not identify individual users.

2. How We Collect Information

We collect information through the following methods:

  • Direct Submission: When you fill out our newsletter subscription form or contact form, you directly provide us with the information requested.
  • Server Logs: Our hosting infrastructure (Vercel) maintains standard server logs that capture technical access data for operational purposes, such as diagnosing server errors, detecting security incidents, and analyzing traffic patterns.
  • Third-Party Services: We use Google Fonts for website typography and Pexels for stock imagery. These services may independently collect usage data in accordance with their own privacy policies. We do not control or have access to data collected by these third parties.

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, analytics scripts, or any form of behavioral tracking. Our Site is intentionally designed to be privacy-respecting and does not engage in user profiling, ad targeting, or cross-site tracking.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain our Services — delivering the content, tools, and features you request.
  • To communicate with you — sending newsletters, updates about new articles or tools, and responses to your inquiries. You may unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time by clicking the "unsubscribe" link in any email or by contacting us directly.
  • To improve our Services — analyzing aggregated usage patterns to understand which content resonates with our audience, and using that insight to create more relevant articles and tools.
  • To ensure security — monitoring for and preventing fraudulent, unauthorized, or illegal activity on our Site.
  • To comply with legal obligations — responding to lawful requests from public authorities, enforcing our terms, and protecting our legal rights.

4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

4.1 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and to provide information to site owners.

4.2 Our Use of Cookies

We do not deploy any cookies for advertising, tracking, or analytics purposes. The only cookies that may be set are:

  • Essential/Session Cookies: These are temporary cookies that may be set by Google Fonts when loading web fonts for proper display. They expire when you close your browser and do not collect personal information.
  • Preference Cookies: If we implement a dark mode toggle or similar user preference feature, a small cookie may be stored locally to remember your preference. This cookie contains no personal data.

4.3 Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings preferences. You can typically configure your browser to reject all cookies, accept only certain cookies, or notify you when a cookie is being set. Please refer to your browser's help documentation for specific instructions. Note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of some websites.

You will be notified via email and/or a prominent notice on our Site of any change in ownership or uses of your personal information.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

  • Newsletter subscriptions: Retained until you unsubscribe or we discontinue the newsletter service.
  • Contact form submissions: Retained for up to 24 months to maintain a record of communications, unless you request earlier deletion.
  • Server logs: Automatically purged by our hosting provider on a rolling basis, typically within 30 days.
  • Tool data: Not retained — all interactive tool calculations are performed locally in your browser and are never transmitted to our servers.

8. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures designed to protect the security of any personal information we process. These measures include:

  • HTTPS encryption: All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted using TLS (Transport Layer Security).
  • Minimal data collection: We intentionally limit the personal data we collect, reducing the risk and impact of any potential security incident.
  • Local processing: Our interactive tools process data entirely within your browser, ensuring sensitive health-related inputs never leave your device.
  • Access controls: Access to any stored personal data is restricted to authorized personnel who require it to perform their duties.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your personal information using commercially acceptable means, we cannot guarantee its absolute security. In the unlikely event of a data breach involving your personal information, we will notify you as required by applicable law.

9. Children's Privacy

Our Services are intended for adults aged 18 and older— namely, parents, expectant parents, and caregivers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child under the age of 13 has provided us with personal information without your consent, please contact us immediately at the email address listed in Section 14. Upon verification, we will promptly delete such information from our records.

We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and do not target our content, tools, or marketing to children under 13.

10. Your Privacy Rights

10.1 General Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access: You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You may request that we correct any inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): You may request that we delete your personal data, subject to certain legal exceptions.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: You may request that we limit how we use your personal data.
  • Right to Data Portability: You may request a copy of your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object: You may object to the processing of your personal data for direct marketing or other purposes.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Where processing is based on your consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal.

10.2 California Residents (CCPA/CPRA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents have the right to:

  • Know what personal information is being collected about them.
  • Know whether their personal information is sold or disclosed and to whom.
  • Say no to the sale of personal information (Note: we do not sell personal information).
  • Access their personal information.
  • Request deletion of their personal information.
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising their privacy rights.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information in Section 14. We will respond to verifiable consumer requests within 45 days as required by law. We may need to verify your identity before processing your request.

10.3 European Economic Area & UK Residents (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, our legal basis for collecting and using your personal information depends on the specific context in which we collect it. We rely on the following legal bases:

  • Consent: You have given clear consent for us to process your personal data for a specific purpose (e.g., subscribing to our newsletter).
  • Legitimate Interests: Processing is necessary for our legitimate interests, such as improving our Services and responding to your inquiries, and those interests are not overridden by your data protection rights.
  • Legal Obligation: Processing is necessary for compliance with a legal obligation to which we are subject.

You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority if you believe your data protection rights have been violated. A list of EU data protection authorities is available at edpb.europa.eu.

12. Do Not Track Signals

Some web browsers offer a "Do Not Track" (DNT) setting that sends a signal to websites requesting that they not track your online activity. Our Site does not respond to DNT signals, because there is currently no universally accepted standard for how such signals should be interpreted and acted upon. However, as noted throughout this policy, we do not engage in behavioral tracking regardless of DNT signal status.
